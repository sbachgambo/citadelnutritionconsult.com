What we offer

Our Services

Clinical expertise combined with practical, culturally-grounded guidance — not quick fixes, sustainable transformation. Every service below is managed as a Services entry in wp-admin once converted.

One-on-One Consultations

Customized guidance from a registered dietitian — tailored meal planning and ongoing support, in-clinic or virtual via Zoom, Google Meet, or WhatsApp video.

Diet Plans & E-Books

Expertly designed nutrition guides tailored to specific goals — weight loss, chronic condition management, meal planning, grocery shopping, and mindful eating.

Online Courses

In-depth video lessons and interactive modules on nutrition, healthy eating, and management of diet-related conditions — self-paced.

Culinary Services

Customized diet calculation and hands-on cooking instruction — recipe modification and meal adaptation for diabetes, weight loss, cancer, kidney disease, and heart conditions while preserving cultural authenticity.

Corporate & Hospital Partnerships

Workplace wellness programs and Medical Nutrition Therapy services for healthcare facilities and gyms without an in-house registered dietitian.

Group Coaching & Follow-Up

Community-based support for clients with shared goals, plus dietitian-led progress monitoring and accountability check-ins.

₦50,000 for a 30-day accountability program (daily follow-ups, meal reviews, weekly sessions)

Therapeutic Food Packs

Customized meal packages for chronic illness recovery, digestive disorders, and weight management — from CNC Smart Foods. See the Shop →

Guest Speaking

Educational presentations on nutrition for community events, churches, corporate gatherings, and hospitals.

Nutritional Assessment

Blood pressure and blood sugar testing plus a BMI checkup — the starting point for any customized plan.
