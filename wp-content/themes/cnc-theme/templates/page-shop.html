Where CNC Smartfoods came from

Draft narrative — please fact-check and edit before publishing. Long before Smartfoods existed, Dietitian Michelle was writing diet plans in clinic and watching clients hit the same wall: they knew what they were supposed to eat, but couldn't find it — or couldn't trust what was on the shelf. Therapeutic eating only works if the right food is actually within reach, so Citadel Nutrition Consult began sourcing and packaging the indigenous ingredients its own clients needed most. That clinical starting point is still what shapes the range today — every product exists because a real diet plan called for it first.
